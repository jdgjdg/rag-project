#### Overview of SQL Clauses
Here is a quick summary of all the clauses we have seen. The computations in the `SELECT` clause now include complicated computations like window functions. Additionally, we have added `GROUP BY` and `HAVING` to the clauses previously discussed.

#### Clause Semantics and Execution Order
While each individual clause is relatively straightforward and easy to understand, the complexity arises in how they combine. There is a strange oddity regarding the `SELECT` clause, which executes at a midpoint between filtering and sorting. Specifically, we perform grouping and filtering on the groups before we execute our computations, as demonstrated in a number of examples.
