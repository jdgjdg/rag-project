To clarify part of the slide at 5:47:  As the video describes, a category type is very similar to a disjoint union, but flipped on its head.  So, when should you use a category vs. a disjoint union?  The ArtCollector example is a good illustration.  One normally would not consider ArtCollector as the disjoint union of Person and Company.  Most importantly, only some persons and some companies are art collectors, and a disjoint union would incorrectly say that they all were.  Additionally, persons and companies are semantically dissimilar with Person and Company having few, if any, shared attributes.  Category types are typically used with ad hoc combinations like ArtCollector.
